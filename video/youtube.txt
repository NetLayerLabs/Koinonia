TITLE
Koinonia: every newcomer in the pew, heard (AI for Church Hackathon 2026)

DESCRIPTION
Every Sunday, someone slips into the back pew with something heavy to carry, and nobody there speaks their language. Koinonia is a two-way pastoral care bridge: a newcomer asks for prayer or help in their own language, a pastor answers in theirs, and a person approves every word.

In this film, recorded on the live app:
- the pew page in 12 languages (Arabic right to left), with the homily read aloud in a native voice
- a prayer request whose name and phone number are removed on the phone before it is sent
- the Care Bridge: an AI summary and draft, a translation checked by an independent back-translation, and a pastor's approval
- the crisis lane: crisis lines at once, a person alerted, no AI reply
- a tradition glossary that keeps each church's own words (하느님 / 하나님, 미사 / 예배)
- live service cues and welcome-team insights

Try it: https://koinonia.netlayerlabs.com
Built for the AI for Church Hackathon 2026, Care & Community track.
AI: Claude through the 0G Compute Network. Voices: ElevenLabs.

A dramatization with stock footage (Mixkit). The app, its screens, its AI replies and its voices are real, recorded on the live site. The churches are sample data with fictional people.
